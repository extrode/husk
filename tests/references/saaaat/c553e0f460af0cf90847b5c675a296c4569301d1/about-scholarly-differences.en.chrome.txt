Below are the dates shown in this app where a scholarly difference exists, with the majority and minority positions from orthodox Sunni sources. OBSERVANCES Laylat al-Baraa'ah The significance of the 15th of Sha'ban is disputed. Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali and others held that the night has merit for individual worship. However, the majority of classical scholars — including Ibn Taymiyyah and al-Nawawi — found the hadith evidence too weak to establish specific collective observances. The Permanent Committee of Saudi Scholars (Fatwa 3491) advises against singling out this night for public ceremonies. Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali, Lata'if al-Ma'arif Ibn Taymiyyah, Iqtida' al-Sirat al-Mustaqim al-Nawawi, al-Majmu' Permanent Committee for Scholarly Research and Ifta', Fatwa 3491 HISTORICAL EVENTS Year of the Elephant — Abraha marches on Makkah The Year of the Elephant is mentioned in the Quran (Surah al-Fil) but no specific month or day is established. Classical scholars agree this was the birth-year of the Prophet ﷺ; al-Tabari and Ibn Hisham record it as ~570 CE. The Hijri year offset (-52 BH) is a modern scholarly estimation and carries inherent uncertainty. Quran 105 (Surah al-Fil) al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah Birth of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ The majority of classical scholars cite 12 Rabi' al-Awwal as the birth date, based on Ibn Ishaq's transmission reported by Ibn Hisham. However, al-Waqidi and others cite 8 Rabi' al-Awwal; Imam Malik ibn Anas and a minority cite the 9th. The year (53 BH / ~570 CE) is widely accepted. Ibn Taymiyyah noted that celebrating the Mawlid as a recurring observance has no basis in the Sunnah. Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Ibn Taymiyyah, Iqtida' al-Sirat al-Mustaqim Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali, Lata'if al-Ma'arif Marriage to Khadijah bint Khuwaylid The year of the marriage is generally placed at 15 years before Prophethood (~595 CE / ~40 BH), when the Prophet ﷺ was 25 years old. Ibn Sa'd records this in Tabaqat al-Kubra. No specific month or day is reliably established in classical sources. Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra First Revelation — Beginning of Prophethood Classical scholars agree the first revelation occurred in Ramadan, based on Quran 2:185 and sound hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari. The 17th of Ramadan is one common estimate; others cite the 21st or 24th. The precise year is placed at 13 BH (approx.), when the Prophet ﷺ was 40 years old. Quran 2:185 Sahih al-Bukhari, Kitab Bad' al-Wahy Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk First Migration to Abyssinia The First Migration to Abyssinia is recorded by Ibn Hisham and Ibn Sa'd as occurring in Rajab, 5 BH. The precise day is not established in classical sources. The number of companions varies between sources: Ibn Hisham cites ~15, Ibn Sa'd cites different counts. Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Death of Khadijah bint Khuwaylid — Year of Grief Khadijah bint Khuwaylid died in the 10th year of Prophethood (3 BH). Ibn Sa'd (Tabaqat al-Kubra) records the date as 10 Ramadan. The year is consistent across major classical sources. The death of Abu Talib occurred the same year; combined, classical scholars called it 'Aam al-Huzn (Year of Grief). Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk al-Israa' wal-Mi'raaj — Night Journey and Ascension The Night Journey and Ascension is established by the Quran (17:1, 53:1-18) and mutawatir hadith. The date of 27 Rajab is a traditional scholarly estimate, not a firmly established fact. Classical scholars disagree on the year: Ibn Hisham places it 18 months before the Hijra; others say 3 years before. Ibn Taymiyyah stated there is no authentic hadith fixing the date to 27 Rajab, and singling it out for celebration is an innovation. Quran 17:1; 53:1-18 Sahih al-Bukhari, Kitab al-Manaqib; Kitab al-Salah Sahih Muslim, Kitab al-Iman Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah Ibn Taymiyyah, Iqtida' al-Sirat al-Mustaqim Second Pledge of Aqabah The Second Pledge of Aqabah occurred in Dhu al-Hijjah, 1 BH. The night of the 13th is the traditional estimate from Ibn Hisham and al-Tabari; no precise day is recorded by all sources. The number of participants — 73 men and 2 women — is well-attested. Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra Battle of the Trench (Khandaq) The Battle of the Trench (Khandaq / al-Ahzab) is placed in 5 AH by the majority of classical scholars including Ibn Hisham and al-Tabari. A minority position places it in 4 AH. The month of Shawwal is the common estimate; no precise single day is recorded. Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani, Fath al-Bari Death of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ The Prophet ﷺ died on 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH — this is the majority position reported by Ibn Hisham, al-Tabari, and Ibn Sa'd. A minority cite the 1st or 2nd of Rabi' al-Awwal. The year 11 AH is unanimously accepted. The day was a Monday, consistent across sound hadith. Sahih al-Bukhari, Kitab al-Maghazi Ibn Hisham, al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra Death of Fatimah bint Muhammad Fatimah bint Muhammad died 6 months after the Prophet ﷺ, placing her death in 11 AH. The precise date is disputed: Ibn Sa'd records 3 Ramadan; others cite Rabi' al-Awwal or other months. The 6-month duration is supported by Sahih al-Bukhari. Her exact burial place is also disputed among scholars. Sahih al-Bukhari, Kitab al-Fara'id Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Martyrdom of Umar ibn al-Khattab Umar ibn al-Khattab was stabbed on 26 Dhu al-Hijjah 23 AH and died a few days later on 1 Muharram 24 AH. Classical sources including al-Tabari and Ibn Sa'd record this consistently. The event marked the end of his 10-year caliphate. The assassin was Abu Lu'lu'ah al-Mujusi (a Zoroastrian slave). al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wal-Muluk Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kubra Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani, al-Isabah fi Tamyiz al-Sahabah
