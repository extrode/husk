Skip to content ← SETTINGS Umm al-Qura Calendar The Umm al-Qura calendar is the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia. Since 2002 (1423 AH) its dates are computed at the Institute of Astronomical & Geophysical Research of the King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (KACST) in Riyadh using modern astronomical theory. RULE IN EFFECT SINCE 1423 AH (15 March 2002 onwards) On the 29th day of the lunar month, if both of the following conditions hold, the next day is the first of the new month: The geocentric conjunction (astronomical new moon) occurs before sunset at Makkah. The Moon sets after the Sun on that evening. Otherwise the current month runs to 30 days. Under this rule the new month always begins after the astronomical new moon, with the lunar crescent above the horizon — though in roughly 75% of cases it remains invisible to the naked eye. EARLIER RULES (historical reference) 1420–1422 AH (April 1999 – March 2002) The month began on the first evening when moonset occurred after sunset at Makkah, regardless of whether the astronomical new moon had yet occurred. Rare anomalous cases (Rajab 1421 AH and Sha'ban 1422 AH) where the moon was near its most northerly ecliptic latitude caused the month to begin before the new moon, prompting the rule change in 1423 AH. 1392–1419 AH (March 1972 – April 1999) The new month began if the astronomical new moon occurred less than 3 hours after Saudi midnight (equivalent to 0h UTC). This corresponds to a lunar age at month-start of roughly 15 hours or less. In about 33.7% of cases the month actually started before the new moon; in nearly all cases (99.3%) the crescent was invisible to the naked eye on the first evening. Before 1392 AH (before March 1972) The rule applied before 1392 AH is uncertain. One claim — that the computed lunar altitude at sunset had to be at least 9° above the horizon at Makkah — is not confirmed by available conversion tables. In about 22% of cases the month started before the astronomical new moon, with the crescent already below the horizon.
