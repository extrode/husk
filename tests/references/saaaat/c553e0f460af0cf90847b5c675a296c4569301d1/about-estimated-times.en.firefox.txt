Skip to content ← SETTINGS Estimated prayer times The app never observes dawn or dusk. Every Fajr and Isha it shows is calculated from the sun's position at your location, using the angle your calculation method sets. ANGLES Angles are conventions. 18° is the end of astronomical twilight, measured by instrument. Observers report true dawn at about 13–16° and the end of the evening red glow at about 12–13°. An earlier Fajr is safer for fasting, a later one safer for prayer. WHEN THE APP ESTIMATES Far from the equator, in the weeks around midsummer the sun does not go far enough below the horizon for the method's angle, or only just reaches it. The sign the angle stands for is then not reliably visible, and the app estimates Fajr and Isha with the high-latitude rule you choose in Settings. The rule is applied as a limit every night: Fajr is never earlier than the rule's Fajr and Isha never later than the rule's Isha, so the times change smoothly from one night to the next. Each high-latitude rule is an estimate the app makes, not something observed. Each estimated time is marked "estimated" in the day view. Open the marker to see how far the sun went below the horizon that night, the angle your method needs, and the arithmetic used. HOW EACH RULE IS DERIVED Angle-Based Fajr no earlier than sunrise − (Fajr angle ÷ 60) of the night; Isha no later than sunset + (Isha angle ÷ 60) of the night. For 18°, that is 18/60 of the night. Midnight Rule Fajr no earlier than the middle of the night (sunset to sunrise); Isha no later than halfway from sunset to Fajr. One-Seventh of Night Fajr no earlier than sunrise − 1/7 of the night; Isha no later than sunset + 1/7 of the night. Applied from latitude 48°; nearer the equator the angle-based rule is used. Nearest Latitude On nights the angle is not reached, Fajr and Isha are computed as at latitude 48°, then kept between this place's own sunrise and Maghrib. On other nights it works as the angle-based rule. Methods that set Isha in minutes after Maghrib (Umm al-Qura, Qatar) have no Isha angle. When those minutes would reach Fajr, Isha is estimated from a 17° angle. Where the sun does not set, or does not rise, all of that day's times are computed as at latitude 48°.
