# 004 First app: saaaat.com

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: accepted (the user).

## Context

Two candidates from [architecture 002](../architecture/002-cloudflare-and-deno.md): saaaat.com
(SvelteKit SPA, needs a client router and `$app/*` shims) and riwalqalb.com's frontend (plain
Svelte, but blocked by its browser floor, decision 003, and eight Vite plugins of its own).

## Decision

saaaat.com's web app (`apps/web`) is the first app on Husk (milestone 2).

## Consequences

- After T6, the next work is the client router over `src/routes/` and the `$app/navigation`,
  `$app/environment`, `$app/state` shims.
- riwalqalb.com stays on its current build until decision 003's add-on exists or its floor moves.
